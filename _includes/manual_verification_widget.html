Step 1 of 2
Have you performed 10+ high-quality reviews from ARR or listed major venues?
⚠️ Venue restriction: Reviewing experience from workshops or unlisted venues will not be accepted. Only reviews from ARR or the major venues listed on this page count toward the 10-review requirement.
⚠️ Evidence requirement: Only completed reviews count as evidence. Incomplete review histories (i.e., active or unfulfilled review assignments) are not eligible.
Step 2 of 2
Do you hold a Master's degree or higher?
Candidates who do not have an awarded Master's degree (including bachelor's students and those in combined Bachelor+Master's or Master's+PhD programs who have not yet officially completed their Master's) should select "No".
Step 2 of 2
Have you served as an ARR secondary reviewer for at least 10 reviews?
Secondary reviewer contributions must have been officially recorded in the ARR reviewing form by your qualified supervisor (capped at 5 reviews per cycle, typically requiring service across at least 2 cycles).
